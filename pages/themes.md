Themes are ready-to-use sets of templates and assets that give your site a complete look and feel, in seconds.

Install a theme with Composer, enable it in your configuration, then customize it by overriding its templates in your own `layouts/` directory.

Looking to add a specific feature rather than change the whole design? Take a look at [Theme Components](/themes/components/).
