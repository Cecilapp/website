Starter kits are complete, ready-to-use websites powered by Cecil: a blog, a documentation site, a podcast, a photo gallery, a links page, a URL shortener, and more.

Pick the one that fits your project, create it with a single Composer command, then make it your own: add your content, adjust the configuration and customize the templates.
