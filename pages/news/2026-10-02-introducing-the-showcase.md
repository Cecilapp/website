The website now has a [**Showcase**](/showcase/) page that lists sites built with Cecil.

It currently features 17 websites: open source projects (such as [Cypht](https://cypht.org) and [libimobiledevice](https://libimobiledevice.org)), community sites, personal blogs and portfolios. Each entry includes a screenshot, a short description and, when available, a link to its source code. You can browse them to get inspiration or to see how others structure their Cecil projects.

### Add your website

Is your website built with Cecil? [Let us know](https://github.com/Cecilapp/Cecil/discussions) and we will add it to the showcase.
