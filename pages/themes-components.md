Theme Components are small, focused themes that add a feature to an existing site without changing its design: turn it into a Progressive Web App, publish a podcast, use Font Awesome or Octicons icons, generate Netlify's `_redirects` and `_headers` files, and more.

They work just like themes: install them with Composer, then enable them in your configuration alongside your main theme. Combine as many as you need.
